Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to report something in Community? Email karthik.vasudevan.10@gmail.com. I usually reply within a day or two, and Community reports are handled within 24 hours.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings in Pixel Deck (the gear at the top of the gallery) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing members get Pro too.

My sprites aren't on my other device

Sprites sync through your own iCloud. On each device: sign in to the same Apple Account, turn on iCloud Drive (Settings › your name › iCloud), and check Settings › iCloud › Sync with iCloud in Pixel Deck. New changes can take a minute to arrive, and need an internet connection.

How do I add a frame or start a new canvas?

Swipe left from the right edge of the canvas to add a frame (the last frame shows underneath as a pink onion skin). Swipe right from the left edge to start a new canvas. Both also have buttons: the + after the frame thumbnails, and the New canvas tile in the gallery.

How does tweening work?

Choose the Select tool, drag over some pixels, then drag the selection along any path and tap Tween. Pixel Deck smooths your path and draws the frames in between.

Community: reporting, blocking and deleting

Open a post and tap Report or Block. Reported posts disappear for you at once, and posts reported by several people are hidden for everyone and reviewed. To delete your own post, open Community, choose Mine, open the post and tap Delete my post. To ask for anything else to be removed, email the address above.
