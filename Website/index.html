Pixel Deck

Draw it. Flip it. Make it move.

Pixel Deck is a pocket-sized pixel-art studio made for animation, on iPhone and iPad. Draw on an endless canvas, swipe left for the next frame with an onion skin underneath, drag pixels along a path and let the app tween the frames in between, then export GIFs, sprite sheets and videos. Share your animations with the Community and remix others'.
