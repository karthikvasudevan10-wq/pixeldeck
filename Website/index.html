For artists

Everything a pixel artist needs.

From a first doodle to a finished loop: a two-minute guided tour gets anyone started, and the tools grow with you.

  • Motion tweeningSelect some pixels and drag them along any path. Pixel Deck smooths your path and draws every frame in between.
  • Brushes, dither and symmetryFour brush sizes, a checkerboard dither brush for shading, and mirror or four-way symmetry.
  • Layers, shapes and transformsEight layers, ten shapes, flip and rotate, copy and paste frames, and trace over any photo.
  • PalettesTen ready-made palettes, your own saved ones, and imports from Lospec and other apps.
  • TimelapseEvery piece records how it was drawn. Replay it, or save it as a video to share.
  • Export anywhereLooping GIFs, MP4 and 4K videos, PNG frames, and sprite sheets with JSON for game engines, all razor sharp.
  • On all your devicesYour art syncs through your own private iCloud to every iPhone and iPad you sign in to.
  • Safe for familiesCommunity is off for anyone under 13, so younger artists can draw and animate freely. No ads, no tracking.

About

Made by someone
who loves pixels.

Hi, I'm Karthik, and I love pixel art.

I've always been amazed at how a handful of squares can feel so alive. My favourite piece of pixel art is the original 16 × 16 Mario. To me it's almost Picasso-like: it abstracts so much character into so few pixels. The moustache. The red cap, with a little hair peeking out at the back. The long nose. Eyes that look studious and full of energy, ready for action. Overalls with golden buckles on the straps, and those shoes. And then it moves: holding his cap as he jumps is a tiny detail that brings the whole character to life.

That's what I love most about pixel art: saying so much with so little. It's why I have a soft spot for single-colour pixel art, where everything rests on shape and light. It's why I love games built from pixels, and that retro feeling of chunky sprites, tiny palettes and little loops that play forever.

Pixel Deck is my way of sharing that love. I want there to be more pixel art in the world, and more people making it. Whether you're picking a colour for the first time, drawing every day, or just enjoying what others make, I hope Pixel Deck makes you smile and nudges you to make something of your own, even if it's only sixteen pixels wide.

Thank you for being here. Say hello any time.
