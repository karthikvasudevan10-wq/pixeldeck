More than a flipbook

Make it move, then share it.

Everything you need to go from a doodle to a looping animation, on any device you own.

  • Motion tweeningSelect some pixels and drag them along any path. Pixel Deck smooths your path and draws every frame in between.
  • Layers and shapesUp to eight layers per sprite, plus lines, rectangles, circles, hexagons, stars and more, outlined or filled.
  • Trace a photoPin any picture under your layers at the opacity you like, and draw over it.
  • Export anywhereLooping GIFs, sprite sheets with JSON for game engines, PNG frames and 1080×1080 videos, all razor sharp.
  • On all your devicesYour sprites sync through your own private iCloud to every iPhone and iPad you sign in to.
  • CommunityShare your animations, browse what others made, and remix them into your own gallery.
