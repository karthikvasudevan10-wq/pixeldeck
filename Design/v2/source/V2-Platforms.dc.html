One app, six shapes

The layout follows the space available (README: "Adaptive layout"). Sprite, frame, tool, colour, onion skin and undo history always carry over, and a stroke in progress during a fold or resize is finished, never lost. iPhone Duo sizes are design points, one third of Apple's screenshot pixels (outer 1398 × 2034, inner 2853 × 2007). Every screen comes in Night (dark) and Paper (light); the night-sky illustrations stay night in both.
