One app, three shapes

Pixel Deck changes its layout as the iPhone Duo opens and folds. Your frame, tool and colour come with you, so nothing reloads.
Closed · 5.4-inch Pocket studio The regular Studio, with controls in a bottom dock for one thumb. Good for quick sketches on the go.
Open flat · 7.6-inch Flipbook spread A real flipbook. The left page shows your last frame, a live playback or a reference image, and you draw on the right page. Swipe left to turn the page. Tools sit by your left thumb, colours by your right.
Half open on a desk Easel The upright half becomes a stage that loops your animation live. The flat half is your drawing desk, so you see each stroke move as you make it.
